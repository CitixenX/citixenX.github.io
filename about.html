About the Author

CitixenX is a moniker adopted by creative writer, visual artist, and poet Rev. Nathan X. Smith, M.A.

Born in Central Kentucky, Smith has always been captivated by stories of distant worlds, remarkable creatures, incredible technology, and more. Naturally, these interests have shaped his writing from an early age—through imaginative stories, compelling characters, and provocative, evocative poetry.

Rev. Smith holds a Bachelor of Arts and a Master of Arts in Communication from the University of Kentucky and West Virginia University. He is also an ordained minister through the Universal Life Church. He currently serves his community as a school teacher and political activist. CitixenX hopes to continue creating for years to come.
